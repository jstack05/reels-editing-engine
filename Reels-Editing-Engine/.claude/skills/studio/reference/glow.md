# glow: the final ship pass

Load `craft-floor.md` and `fidelity.md` first. `glow` is the last look before export, the one that catches the
small misses a busy edit leaves behind. Every other verb hands off here. It is not a redesign and it never
changes the creative direction; it confirms the reel clears the floor and reports honestly what, if anything,
is left for the creator to do.

## What it does

A quick, scored pass, borrowed lightly from impeccable's audit idea but reel-native and not ADA-strict. Run
the reel against the floor and the soft rules and report a short verdict, not a lecture:

**Hard floor (must pass, from `craft-floor.md`):**
- Every element inside the safe zone (x 150-930, y 270-1620, from `product/safe_zones.py`). Nothing on the face. Nothing off
  screen.
- Every line readable over the footage it rides (check the busiest and lightest frames).

**Soft read (flag, do not force):**
- Hook is clearly the loudest thing; caption is clearly subordinate.
- Leading breathes but stays one thought; no cramped or drifting stacks.
- One accent, one word or phrase. Case and weight match the pack.
- Spacing has rhythm; nothing crowds the head or an edge.

## Report honestly per path (from `fidelity.md`)

This is the point of `glow`: it never claims a render is finished when part of it is the creator's in-app job.

- **Well-done MP4:** confirm the pixels. If it clears the floor, it is ready to post.
- **Medium (CapCut + `.mov`):** confirm the overlay pixels; note it moves as a block.
- **Raw (editable CapCut):** confirm the engine text, then LIST the two or three in-app nudges left, plainly,
  so nothing ships wrong by accident. Typical list: "run Auto Captions, keep them just under the chin inside
  the safe band; check the hook did not reflow when you edited it." The reel is not sloppy, and the creator
  knows exactly what is theirs to finish.

## The tone

Big-sister-honest. Say what is great, name what is left, in a sentence or two. Never a wall of findings. The
creator should feel the reel is ready, or know the one or two moves to get it there.
