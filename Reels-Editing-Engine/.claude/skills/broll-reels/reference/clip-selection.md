# Clip selection + optional b-roll ideas (tailored to the creator's niche)

The creator already filmed their b-roll. This skill does NOT ask them to film anything. This doc does two
jobs: (1) the CORE job — pick which of their existing clips work as text-over reels, and (2) a BONUS,
opt-in helper — if their folder is thin and they want more, suggest b-roll ideas **for their specific
niche.**

## 1. Which of their EXISTING clips are good candidates (core)
- **Vertical 9:16**, roughly 5s of usable action (trim longer clips down).
- **Negative space for the hook** — some empty wall / counter / surface where text can sit off the subject.
- **No eye contact with the lens** (looking at a task / window / screen reads best; a clip staring at camera is weaker).
- **Locked-ish framing** — minimal camera shake; the motion is the subject, not the camera.
- **A real, lived-in background** (window, workspace, warm room). Skip stark-white/empty-void clips.
- **Natural, not slow-mo, not overly HD/cinematic** — casual phone footage reads more authentic.

## Placement per clip
Text goes where the subject ISN'T: subject high in frame → hook lower / center; subject centered → hook up
top. The baker's `--pos top|center` handles this; pick per clip so text never covers the face/subject.

## 2. Optional b-roll ideas — TAILORED TO THEIR NICHE (only if they want help)
Not part of the required flow. Offer it only if their folder is thin or they ask "what else should I film?"
**The ideas MUST fit their niche** — pull the niche from their Brand Homebase / `brand-kit.md`. Never hand a
creator a shot list from a different niche. Translate the SHAPE of a good b-roll clip into their world:
- **A hands-at-work shot** — the core action of their craft, framed with negative space.
  - baker → hands shaping dough / piping / pulling a tray. music teacher → hands on the keys / tuning.
    home designer → laying out fabric swatches / hanging art. coach → writing on a whiteboard / on a call.
- **A quiet environment shot** — their workspace with room to breathe (studio, kitchen, desk, site).
- **A "getting ready / setting up" shot** — the small ritual before the work (aprons on, opening the shop, laptop open).
- **A detail / result shot** — the finished thing or a close detail (the plated bake, the arranged room, the sheet music).
Keep the SAME quality bar as section 1 (9:16, negative space, no lens eye-contact, natural not cinematic).

## If a folder is thin / clips are weak
Use the strongest candidates, reuse a good clip across more than one hook, and flag (don't silently drop)
any hooks that had no fitting clip so the creator knows what's uncovered. You can offer the niche-tailored
ideas above. Never invent or generate footage — this is the bring-your-own-footage engine.
