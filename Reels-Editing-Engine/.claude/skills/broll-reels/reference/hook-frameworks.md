# Hook frameworks (Edit Engine — bundled)

Read this before writing any hook. These are the proven SHAPES that stop the scroll. This ships with the Edit Engine.

## The one rule
A hook is a **promise the reel keeps** and its only job is to earn the next 3 seconds. It must do ONE of these — never a soft affirmation:
- **Specific painful scenario** the viewer recognizes ("it's 2am and you're doing the math on whether you can afford to come home").
- **Open curiosity loop** ("you don't need a business plan. you need to answer one question first.").
- **Concrete payoff promise** — a number, a list, a how, a proof ("i booked 12 clients in 30 days with one system. here's how...").

BANNED shape: comfort/permission platitudes — "you're allowed to...", "you can...", "you don't need X, just Y", "it's not a fantasy". They resolve the feeling instead of creating tension. If it reads like an inspirational quote, rewrite it.

## The 8 structures (pick 2-4 per idea, spread the angles)
1. **Educational / Tutorial** — "how i [got the result] with only [the constraint], start to finish." / "the [number]-minute version of [the thing people overcomplicate]." Show a system/how-to.
2. **Secret Reveal** — "the unglamorous part of [the result everyone wants]." / "the part of [the thing] that never makes it on camera." A hidden truth.
3. **Contrarian / Negative** — "[common advice] is the worst move." / "i stopped [the thing everyone swears by] and [the result] got better." Pick a fight with the default.
4. **Raw Shock** — a claim/number so surprising it stops the scroll. "i didn't believe [the result] either, until [the proof]." / "this took [a short, specific amount of time] and it changed [the big thing]."
5. **Question** — "what would you do if [X]?" / "how long have you been waiting to [do the thing]?" Open a loop the reel closes.
6. **Experimentation** — "i gave [X] [a set amount of time]. the results surprised me." / "day [number] of [the experiment]. here's what's changed so far."
7. **Fortuneteller** — belief-shift / future: "if you're where i was [time] ago, here's what's coming next." / "in [time] you'll look back at [this exact moment] and [the surprising feeling]."
8. **Comparison** — "what i thought [X] would look like. what it actually looks like." / "[the old way] took me [long time]. [the new way] takes [short time]."

Pour the creator's theme into a proven shape — keep the shape's rhythm, swap in her specifics.

## Write to the BUYER'S niche
Every creator has their own niche. Pull the niche, audience, voice, and differentiator from the creator's
**Brand Homebase / brand-kit.md** and write every hook to THAT world — a baker, a music teacher, a home
designer, a coach. Speak to the specific person their content is for. Any example in this doc is just an
illustration of the SHAPE; never carry another niche's language into a creator's reels.

## Voice + brand rules (pull specifics from the creator's Brand Homebase / brand-kit.md)
- **Built out + stranger-clear.** Not a 3-word theme. A stranger in their niche feels seen in 2 seconds.
- **Mix formats** — some `POV:`, some statement, some question, some confessional. Not all one shape.
- **Accent turn** — a small reframe/payoff in the pack's accent font, only where a line earns it. On teaching/proof/list hooks, end it with a caption-pointer: "here's how...", "here's what i'd do instead...", "5 signs it's you...".
- **Carry THEIR differentiator** — the specific thing that sets the creator apart, never a generic category word. (A baker's might be "from scratch, no shortcuts"; a coach's "without the burnout"; a teacher's "no theory, just play." Find theirs in the Brand Homebase.)
- **Never martyr / complaining** — frame the want as a positive invitation, never a burden or a grievance.
- **Speak TO the audience** — address the viewer directly ("you"), name people the way that niche does; avoid cold third-person ("them") where a warmer direct reference fits.
- **Spell it out for strangers** — no insider shorthand a scroller wouldn't parse in 2 seconds.
- **No em dashes.** Periods and short sentences.
- **Balanced treatment** — the baker auto-balances lines to equal width; pass plain text, no manual line breaks.

## Format model (from the creator's own top carousels/reels)
Big statement + small subhead is the winning shape, e.g.:
`POV: the career you chose at 22 feels like a trap at 32`  ·  subhead `5 signs it's you`
Headline = the pack's headline font, subhead/turn = the pack's accent font, in the pack's text color. The
fonts come from the creator's chosen STYLE PACK — no font is hardcoded and none ships with this skill
(licensed brand fonts are never distributed). See SKILL.md → Style pack for how the pack resolves to
fonts, and for the baker.
