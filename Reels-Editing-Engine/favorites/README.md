# ⭐ Your Favorites

This folder is for the things you love using in your reels. Drop them in and they show up **first** in every
Swap menu when you review a reel, ahead of everything the engine comes with.

- **sounds/** for sound effects
- **music/** for songs to play under your voice
- **gifs/** for GIFs
- **graphics/** for stickers, logos and other see-through images
- **photos/** for photos (covers, carousels, stories)
- **broll/** for b-roll clips you cut away to over your words
- **fonts/** for fonts you want in your own style packs

Easiest of all: drop anything in your **inbox** folder and the engine files a copy in the right drawer for you.

Just drag files from Finder (or File Explorer) into the right folder. That's it. There's nothing to set up and
nothing to say. If you put something in the wrong folder, no problem: the engine sorts it out for you.

Your files stay right here on your computer. They are never uploaded, never shared, and an update never touches
them. Your originals are never changed: when the engine needs a different version of something (a GIF for a
finished video, say), it makes its own copy and leaves yours alone.
