# 🎬 Favorite b-roll

Clips you like to cut away to: your coffee, your desk, your kids' hands on the counter. A cutaway fills the screen
over a few of your words while your voice keeps going. The engine picks each clip's best moment, and you can
slide it to another moment, move it a word earlier or later, or turn its sound on.

Drop clips here, or leave them in your inbox. Your originals are never changed.
