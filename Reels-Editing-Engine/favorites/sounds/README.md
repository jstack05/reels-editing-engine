# 🔊 Favorite sounds

Drop the sounds you love here: pops, clicks, whooshes, dings, your signature sound, a song you have the rights to.

- **Sound effects** (anything 15 seconds or shorter) show up first when you swap a sound on a reel.
- **Songs** (anything longer) show up first when you pick the music under a reel.

Files that work: **.mp3, .wav, .m4a**, and also .aac, .aif, .flac, .ogg.

Every sound you swap in is leveled for you, so it sits at the right loudness under your voice. No volume
fiddling needed.

Only add sounds you're allowed to use in your reels (ones you made, bought, or got with a license).
