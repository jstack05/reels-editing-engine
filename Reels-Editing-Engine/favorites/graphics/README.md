# 🎨 Favorite graphics

Drop your stickers, logos, doodles and other images here.

Files that work: **.png** (best with a see-through background), **.svg**, and a **.mov** with a see-through
background (exported as ProRes 4444).

A few that won't work, and what to do instead:
- **.jpg** photos can't be see-through, so they'd sit on your video as a box. Save them as .png instead.
- **.mp4** videos are footage, not graphics. Put b-roll in your reel's b-roll folder instead.
- **Design files** (.psd, .ai, .pdf): export them as .png first.

If something can't be used, the engine tells you which file and why, so you're never left guessing.
