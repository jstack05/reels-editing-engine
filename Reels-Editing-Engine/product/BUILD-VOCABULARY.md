# What to Call It — your build vocabulary

The whole point of this engine is that you direct and I build. That works best when you know the words for
what you want. This is your cheat sheet. Skim it once, keep it open while you build, and use it to tell me
exactly what to do. You never have to memorize it, and plain talk always works too ("make that word huge,
one at a time" is just as good as "make it a takeover"). But when you want to be fast, say the name.

Every line here is a thing you can ask for. Format: **the name** = what it is → *say this.*

---

## The format (what kind of reel this is)
You don't have to pick: your footage tells me (you talking on camera is a Yap). Say one of these if you want a
different one.

- **Yap** = you on camera, talking. One take, cut down. → *"let's make a Yap."*
- **Voiceover** = faceless. Your voice over your own b-roll, cut to feel cinematic. → *"make my voiceover reel."*
- **Animation** = no camera at all. A script becomes designed animated frames on your voiceover or music. → *"make an animation reel."*
- **B-Roll Reels** = a folder of b-roll you already filmed, turned into a batch of hooked, post-ready reels. → *"make b-roll reels."*

## How cooked you want it (doneness)
This decides how much you finish versus how much I finish. Ask early.

- **Raw** = an editable CapCut draft, plain text you can retype, the most hands-on. → *"keep it raw."*
- **Medium** = designed layers you can still slide and hide, on an editable timeline. → *"make it medium."*
- **Well-done** = a finished video, fully designed, baked in, ready to post. → *"make it well-done."*
- **Hands-off** = I run the whole thing start to finish and hand you a finished video. → *"do it hands-off."*

## Your look (style packs)
Choose once, reuse forever. Some fonts come from CapCut Pro.

- **Editorial** = classic and elegant. **Playful** = bold and fun. **Butter** = soft and buttery. → *"use Playful."*
- **Build your own** = your fonts, your two colors, a vibe, a name, made into a real pack you keep. → *"/studio recipe"* or *"build my own pack."*

## Captions (how your words show up on screen)
Four ways. You can mix them in one reel, line by line.

- **Single-word captions** = one big word at a time, centered. Punchy. → *"make these single-word captions."* (or *"/studio single [line]"*)
- **Karaoke captions** = the whole line sits on screen and each word lights up as you say it. → *"make this karaoke."* (or *"/studio karaoke [line]"*)
- **Takeover (full-screen)** = the words stack and fill the screen, the key word lit, for one big emphasis beat. → *"make this a takeover."* (or *"/studio takeover [line]"*)
- **Native CapCut captions** = CapCut's own Auto Captions, the bold-white-with-yellow-keyword look, run in the app. → *"I'll do captions in CapCut."*

## On-screen graphics (the extras that dress it up)
Bring more of these on a teaching reel, almost none on a confessional one.

- **Hook** = the headline card up top that stops the scroll. → *"put the hook here."*
- **Thought bubble** = a little handwritten aside or comment, in the hand-drawn font. → *"add a thought bubble that says…"*
- **Stat card / count-up** = a number that animates on, like $300 ticking up. → *"make that a count-up."*
- **Breakaway card** = a full-screen designed card that breaks away from your footage for a beat. → *"add a breakaway card here."*
- **B-roll cutaway** = a clip that cuts away while your voice keeps going. → *"cut away to b-roll on this line."*
- **Text behind you** = your hook sitting behind your head, so you pass in front of it. → *"put the hook behind me."* (or *"/studio behind hook"*)
- **Cutout** = you cut out and placed over a background or card. → *"cut me out over this."*
- **Second camera (PIP)** = a second phone filming the same moment (usually the other person's face), lined up with yours by sound and shown as a small picture over your clips. Their mic carries their lines, yours carries yours. → *"sync my second camera"* / *"add his camera as a PIP."*
- **Doodles / stickers** = your own art, placed and animated. (You bring the art, I place it.) → *"drop my star doodle on this word."*

## Motion and sound
- **Zoom-in opener** = a slow push-in on your first line to pull people in. → *"start with a slow zoom."*
- **Punch-in** = a quick zoom on an accent beat. → *"punch in on that word."*
- **Matched sound effects** = little sounds that land on the motion (a click on a bubble, a tick on a type-on). → *"add matched sound effects."*
- **Your sound palette** = a CapCut project that holds only the sound effects you love. I study it and reach
  for those first, every reel. Tell me your don'ts too (vary them, never twice in a row). → *"study my CapCut
  draft called my sound palette."*
- **Music bed** = free background music made right on your Mac, laid quiet under your voice. → *"add a music bed."* (or *"skip the music."*)

## Finishing touches (the /studio commands)
Type **/studio** any time to see the full menu. The ones you'll reach for most:

- **/studio hush [thing]** = make one thing smaller/quieter. **/studio punch [thing]** = make it bigger/bolder.
- **/studio font** = fix text size and wrapping. **/studio tidy** = fix spacing and placement. **/studio accent** = fix a color that isn't reading.
- **/studio vibe** = one tasteful personality touch. **/studio ultra vibe** = stack every touch that fits (max styling).
- **/studio single / karaoke / takeover [line]** = switch one line's caption treatment (see Captions above).
- **/studio behind** = text behind you. **/studio recipe** = build your own pack. **/studio glow** = the final ship pass.
- Full list + what each gives you: the **Studio Command Vault** (in your course resources).

## Your cover
- **Cover / thumbnail** = the still that shows in the grid. I pull your best frames by expression, you pick, then we add a title. → *"make my cover."*

## Say it in the footage (the shortcut)
You can direct the edit while you film. Say the effect's name on the line you want it on, pointing at the
line itself: *"this is where the hook goes," "this line is a karaoke caption," "here's a count-up to five
thousand dollars," "and this is a takeover."* I hear it and build that effect on that line. It keeps the build
short because nothing gets guessed. Your first reel script (`product/FIRST-REEL-SCRIPT.md`) is made entirely of
these, so it shows you the whole menu on your own face.

## Making it YOURS (the magic phrases)
These are how the engine learns you, so every reel needs fewer tweaks than the last.

- *"teaching or confessional?"* = the one question I ask that decides how loaded the reel gets. Teaching = more graphics; confessional = clean and restrained.
- *"learn my pattern for the next reel"* = take the change I just made and make it my permanent default.
- *"sort my inbox"* = file everything I dropped in my inbox (sounds, songs, GIFs, graphics, photos, fonts) into my engine folder. It copies, so I can clear my inbox after.
- *"make a pack from my design"* = turn my DESIGN.md (in `favorites/designs/<name>/`, with its fonts) into my own style pack, plus any new looks it describes. The format: `product/DESIGN-MD-SPEC.md`.
- *"switch my reel to Editorial"* (any pack) = every look I picked from the lookbook redraws in that pack, same moments. Same as the Lookbook's pack tabs > Use this pack.
- *"looks"* = my cut is done: plan and draw my looks so they play on my page.
- *"render"* or *"export"* = make my reel now, the same as pressing Export on the Finish tab.
- *"one-shot edit"* / *"autopilot"* / *"just edit it"* = edit it all the way to a finished CapCut draft or MP4 with no stops, then open my page so I can change anything.
- *"update my edit"* = do everything my page is waiting on Claude for (my notes, looks to redo on my new cut, new-look ideas). The page's "Your preview is waiting on N changes" card copies this line for me.
- *"build my look"* = design a brand-new look with me, from the idea I saved on the page (Lookbook > Build a new look), right on my footage. When I love it, it joins my lookbook for any line, any reel.
- *"remember this"* / *"make this my default"* / *"always do it this way"* = save a preference for good.
- *"turn off the chime"* / *"turn the chime back on"* = the soft sound when Claude is waiting on me (a question, or a permission to allow).
- *"make trial reels"* / *"test my hook"* = 3 real re-edits of my finished reel (I pick which): a later line moved to the front, some lines left out, a new hook, one version each, to post as Instagram trial reels and see which opening pulls. My look stays mine. A reel I finished in CapCut is re-cut from my own CapCut draft, every layer moving with my words, and the hook copies one I styled myself.
- *"teach my style from this reel"* = turn the notes I gave on this reel into rules for my next ones. I tick the ones to keep. It also comes up on its own at the end of every reel I gave notes on ("stop offering that" turns it off).
- *"add that to my word list"* = teach me a caption spelling (a name, a brand, a word I keep mishearing).

## Before it renders (the beat sheet)
- **Beat sheet** = every key moment of your reel as a numbered still with a note stamped on it, shown before
  anything renders. → *"show me the beat sheet."* I offer it on every build.
- **Notes by number** = change just one moment. → *"beat 4, make the hook smaller."* / *"beat 9, different photo."*
- **Sandbox a beat** = see one moment moving before the whole reel renders. → *"sandbox beat 7."*
- **Always / never** = *"always show me the beat sheet"* skips the question; *"stop offering the beat sheet"* turns it off.

## Reviewing your cut (the visual editor or the original widget)
- **The visual editor** (beta) = the upgrade to the widget: your whole reel on one page in your browser. Tap a word
  and press Cut, hear it instantly, swap looks and sounds, export. → *"open my visual editor"* or *"show me the
  visual editor."* Can't find it, or it didn't open? Type either one any time, in any chat: it opens your most
  recent reel (name a reel to open that one).
- **The original widget** = your style plan right in the chat, like the course videos. → *"show me the original
  widget."* (*"text only"* works too.)
- **Make it stick** = *"always use the visual editor"* / *"always use the widget"* skips the question; *"ask me each
  time"* brings it back.
- **Show me around** = replays the editor's quick tips, right on the page.
- **Trim** = take dead space out, right on the page's timeline, like CapCut: drag a clip's edge, or put the playhead
  where the dead space ends and press **Cut before playhead** (Q) / **Cut after playhead** (W). A pause in the middle
  of a line → **Split** (B) at the playhead, then drag the two edges together. Zoom in (+) for precision, and drag the
  grip on the timeline's top edge up for a taller timeline. → *"how do I trim the dead space?"*
- **How do I…?** = ask me anything about the editor page; I know every button on it.
- **Sandbox** = picture a look that isn't in your lookbook? Press **Sandbox** in Visuals and say it, any lines, any
  moment (*"on lines 4 and 5, a count-up card behind my head"*), then type *"sandbox"* here. Three quick sketches show
  up side by side: lock one in (for every reel, or just this one) or remix it for another round.
