# 📥 Your Reel Inbox

Drop the clips you want to edit **right here**. That's the whole system.

When you're ready, say **"edit this reel"** in Claude Code and it grabs the newest clip in this folder,
copies it into a fresh project (your original stays safe here), and starts your rough cut.

**One-time tip:** drag this `inbox` folder into your Finder sidebar (just drag the folder onto the left
panel of any Finder window). Now it's one click from anywhere. Drop a clip in, say "edit this reel," done.

You can drop more than one clip. "Edit this reel" takes the newest. Say "edit the clip called ___" to pick a
specific one.

**Making B-Roll Reels?** Make a folder called **b-roll** in here and drop your clips in it, long footage is
fine. Say **"prep my b-roll"** to cut it into the best short clips, then **"make b-roll reels"** to put your
hooks on and get a numbered posting queue. Your originals stay right here, untouched.

## Getting footage off your phone (one-time setup)

Film on your phone's real camera app, then bring the clips over at full quality. You only set this up once.

1. Plug your iPhone into your Mac.
2. Open **Image Capture** (it's already on your Mac, no install needed).
3. Pick your iPhone in the left sidebar.
4. Bottom left, click the little arrow to expand the options.
5. Set **"Import To:"** to this `inbox` folder.
6. *(Optional)* Check **"Delete after import"** so your phone clears out as it copies.
7. Click **Import All**, or select just the clips you want and click **Import**.

After that it's: film, plug in, hit Import, then come back and say "edit this reel."

**Filmed in Cinematic mode?** Your phone makes two files per clip, and they look identical in Finder.
The one named with an extra `E` (like `IMG_E1234.MOV`) is the finished one with the blurry background
baked in. The other one is the raw capture, where that blur is still just data your Mac's editor can't
read. Drop the `E` one in here. Claude checks for this too and will tell you if it spots the raw one,
so you can't get it wrong by accident.

**Keep this folder tidy.** Once a reel is cut, move that clip out to wherever you keep finished
footage. "Edit this reel" grabs the newest clip, so a tidy inbox means it always grabs the right one.
Your originals are never moved or changed by the engine, so it's safe to clear them out yourself
whenever you want.
